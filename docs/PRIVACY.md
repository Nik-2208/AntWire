# AntWire Privacy, Telemetry & Zero-PII Policy

> **Created & Developed by [Nikhilesh H. Chavda](https://nik-portfolio-lime.vercel.app/)**  
> *AntWire Open Source Platform — Transparent, Zero-Tracking Architecture*

---

## 1. Zero-PII Guarantee

AntWire is designed with an uncompromising commitment to user privacy:

* **No Personal Data Collection**: We **never** collect, store, or transmit names, email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, geolocation coordinates, or browser fingerprints.
* **No Third-Party Ad Trackers**: AntWire contains zero commercial tracking scripts, ad networks, or invasive analytics pixels.
* **Local-First Execution**: All 55,000 neurons, spiking activation dynamics, sensory perception rays, and reinforcement learning routines run **100% locally on your machine**.

---

## 2. Anonymous Telemetry & Download Metrics

To maintain aggregate open-source project statistics (e.g. total model downloads, active community feedback items), AntWire utilizes randomly generated, non-fingerprinting session tokens:

* **Anonymous Session ID**: A randomly generated UUID token (e.g. `anon-a1b2c3d4-e5f6...`) stored strictly in your browser's local storage. It does **not** identify your hardware, IP address, or person.
* **Download Events**: When downloading an official `.antbrain` model package, an event containing `{ modelId, modelVersion, anonymousSessionId, timestamp, source }` is recorded to prevent spam counters.

---

## 3. User Controls & Opt-Out

You maintain full control over your telemetry preferences:

1. **Opt-Out Toggle**: Open the **Community** tab $\to$ **Privacy & Telemetry** section and toggle *"Opt-Out of Non-Essential Download Telemetry"*.
2. **Session Regeneration**: Click *"Regenerate Token"* at any time to purge and replace your anonymous session token with a new random identifier.
3. **Offline Mode**: If network connectivity is severed or cloud services are blocked, AntWire functions fully offline in Local Mode without interruption.
