# ANT BRAIN — Colony Superorganism Model

## 1. Decentralized Collective Cognition
An ant colony is an emergent superorganism. There is **no centralized commander** or executive orchestrator. The reproductive Queen does not issue tactical directives; instead, worker task decisions emerge from:
1. Local sensory cues (pheromones, tactile contacts, food odors).
2. Internal motivational drives (energy, hunger, fatigue, fear).
3. Individual role tendencies and temporal polyethism.
4. Colony-level homeostatic demands.

---

## 2. The 10-Dimensional Colony Needs Vector
The colony evaluates a normalized 10-dimensional vector $\mathbf{N} \in [0, 1]^{10}$:

| Dimension | Description | Primary Drivers |
|---|---|---|
| `foodNeed` | Foraging pressure | Food storage deficit vs. colony demographic reserve target |
| `waterNeed` | Hydration demand | Ambient temperature and colony fluid balance |
| `broodNeed` | Nursing pressure | Count of un-fed larvae and unattended eggs |
| `queenNeed` | Queen support | Somatic energy state and egg-laying demand of the Queen |
| `nestNeed` | Nest excavation/repair | Nest chamber overcrowding and structural damage |
| `defenseNeed` | Predator repulsion | Proximity and count of active arthropod predators |
| `sanitationNeed` | Corpse removal | Dead workers inside or near the nest perimeter |
| `socialCareNeed` | Trophallaxis & Rescue | Starving workers emitting local help cues |
| `explorationNeed` | Scouting & mapping | Territory mapping and food trail discovery |
| `reserveNeed` | Buffer preservation | Safeguarding emergency nutrition reserves |

---

## 3. Worker Caste & Role Taxonomy
Workers maintain a persistent primary role separate from their immediate task objective:
- **FORAGER**: Locates, harvests, and transports resource morsels.
- **SCOUT**: Explores frontier boundaries with elevated movement speed.
- **NURSE**: Tends and feeds developing eggs, larvae, pupae, and the Queen.
- **BUILDER**: Excavates new subterranean galleries and repairs nest walls.
- **GUARD**: Patrols colony perimeter and repels intruding predators.
- **SANITATION**: Carries deceased nestmates away to surface refuse middens.
- **GENERAL_WORKER**: Adaptive reserve capable of dynamic task switching.
