## 2. Biological Distinction & Scientific Transparency

AntWire maintains complete transparency regarding the nature of its neural and colony simulations:

```text
┌─────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Evidence Tier           │ Scientific Definition                                  │
├─────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [BIOLOGICAL FACT]       │ Empirically verified in peer-reviewed entomology.      │
│ [BIOLOGICALLY INSPIRED] │ Structural motif modeled after insect brain regions.   │
│ [COMPUTATIONALLY        │ Mathematical representations (LIF dynamics, spatial    │
│  MODELLED]              │ pheromone diffusion, PPO policy optimization).         │
│ [SPECULATIVE]           │ Exploratory computational hypotheses.                  │
└─────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

> **Key Distinction**: AntWire is an independent computational research project and does **not** claim to provide an experimentally reconstructed complete biological ant connectome. References to projects such as FlyWire ([https://flywire.ai/](https://flywire.ai/)) serve solely as conceptual inspiration for web-based neural exploration.

## 3. Authoritative Model Releases

### AntWire 55K Synaptic Foundation Model (`v2.5.0-prod`)
* **Neuron Count**: 55,420 Leaky Integrate-and-Fire (LIF) units across 5 primary insect neuropils:
  * Antennal Lobes (AL): 4,800 microglomeruli & projection neurons
  * Mushroom Body (MB): 24,000 Kenyon cells & MB output neurons
  * Central Complex (CX): 8,400 compass, bridge, and fan-shaped neurons
  * Subesophageal Zone (SEZ): 6,200 premotor and feeding circuits
  * Ventral Nerve Cord (VNC): 12,020 thoracic & abdominal motor units
* **Synaptic Count**: 184,500 calibrated synaptic weights with Spike-Timing-Dependent Plasticity (STDP) and Dopamine/Octopamine modulation.
