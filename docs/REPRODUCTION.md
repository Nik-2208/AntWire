# ANT BRAIN — Reproductive Biology & Holometabolous Lifecycle

## 1. Queen Oviposition Kinetics
The Queen's reproductive output is governed by metabolic input:
- Consumes stored colony nutrition for somatic maintenance and oogenesis.
- Lays eggs into the Brood Nursery at rates modulated by ambient temperature and nourishment.
- Somatic energy deficit pauses oviposition.

---

## 2. Holometabolous Development Stages
Brood undergoes complete metamorphosis:
1. **EGG**: Non-motile; requires nurse grooming and optimal temperature ($24\text{–}28^\circ\text{C}$).
2. **LARVA**: High protein & sugar requirement; feeds via trophallaxis from nurse workers to accumulate developmental mass.
3. **PUPA**: Non-feeding cocoon stage; undergoes final somatic differentiation into adult worker or reproductive caste.
4. **ADULT**: Newly emerged worker joins the active colony workforce with initial inside-nest polyethism bias.
